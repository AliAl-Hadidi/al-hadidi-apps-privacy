Chain Escape: Puzzle Game Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · App: Chain Escape: Puzzle Game (ali.alhadidi.chain_escape) · Publisher: Al-Hadidi Apps · Contact: alhadidi.apps@gmail.com

In short: Chain Escape has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. It shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choices at any time in the game.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores the following only on your device:

  • Your progress: stars per level and the puzzle in progress, so you can continue later.
  • Your settings: sound, theme and your usage-analytics choice.
  • Simple gameplay counters, such as levels started or completed and hints used, which help balance difficulty.

We do not have access to this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the level map, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads that unlock a solution demonstration. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information;
  • interactions with ads.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent and settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Google › Ads or Privacy › Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Consent management

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before ads are requested. You can review or change your choices at any time using Ad privacy options, found in the game under About (the "i" button on the level map). Your choices also control the data-storage settings used by Google Analytics for Firebase.

4. Usage analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are started, completed or abandoned, how often hints and solutions are used, and how long levels take. This helps us improve difficulty and fix problems. Firebase also collects:

  • standard app and device information, such as app version, device model, operating system version, country and language;
  • an app-instance identifier;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address.

The game does not send your name, email or any text you type. You can switch usage analytics off at any time under About › Usage analytics.

5. Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the bug. The report contains the technical error trace, device model, operating system version, app version and the state of the app at the time of the crash. Crash reporting follows the same Usage analytics switch.

6. Google Play features

The game can show Google Play's in-app review prompt and in-app update prompt. These are provided by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive your review or account details.

8. Children

Chain Escape is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and your choices

  • Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics according to the retention period configured for the game, with a maximum of 14 months. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • You can turn off usage analytics and crash reports in the game, change ad consent under Ad privacy options, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. For data held by Google, you can also use your Google account controls.
