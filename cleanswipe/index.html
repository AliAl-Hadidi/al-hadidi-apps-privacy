CleanSwipe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Publisher: Al-Hadidi Apps · Contact: alhadidi.apps@gmail.com

CleanSwipe helps you find duplicate and similar photos, old screenshots and large videos so you can free up space. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your photos and videos are analysed on your phone and are never uploaded. The app shows ads from Google, as described below. It has no account, and we, the publisher, receive no data from the app itself.

1. Your photos and videos

  • Access. With your permission, CleanSwipe reads the photos and videos on your phone (Android's "Photos and videos" permission; on Android 12 and earlier, the storage read permission). On Android 14 and later you can give access to selected items only.
  • On-device analysis. CleanSwipe compares files on your phone to find exact copies and similar shots and to suggest which one to keep. This happens entirely on the device.
  • Never uploaded. CleanSwipe never uploads, copies to a server, or shares your photos, videos, thumbnails, file names, folder names, image fingerprints or their contents.
  • Private index on your phone. To work quickly, CleanSwipe keeps an index in its private app storage: file sizes, dates, dimensions, folder and file names, image fingerprints and quality measures, your review marks, and a history of space freed. It is excluded from Android backups and device transfers. It is deleted when you choose "Reset CleanSwipe data" in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Deleting items. CleanSwipe only moves items to Android's trash after you confirm in Android's own dialog; you can restore them for about 30 days. Items are deleted permanently only if you choose "Delete forever" and confirm.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, CleanSwipe can show a weekly reminder about new clutter. It is created on your phone; nothing is sent to us to produce it. You can turn it off in Settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CleanSwipe is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your choice with Google's consent tool before ads are requested. Where such a choice applies, Settings shows "Privacy choices (ads)" so you can change it at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. On Android versions that support it, Google's ads software may also use Android's own ad services (the Privacy Sandbox, for example ad topics and ad measurement), which you can manage in the ad privacy section of your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. No analytics or crash reporting in this version

This version of CleanSwipe contains no analytics, crash-reporting or remote-configuration software (such as Google Firebase). The app sends no usage statistics or crash reports to us or to anyone else. If a future version adds such a service, we will update this policy, including what is collected and how long it is kept, before that version is released.

5. Contacting us

If you write to us at alhadidi.apps@gmail.com, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write, and we use them to reply to you. The app pre-fills the subject with its name and version number; nothing else is attached. You can ask us to delete our correspondence with you at any time. The Facebook and Instagram links in the app's "About & contact" section open those services, whose own privacy policies apply.

6. What CleanSwipe does not do

  • No account or sign-in; we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or messages.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Everything in the app works offline. Without a connection, ads simply do not load.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: delete it with Settings → "Reset CleanSwipe data", or by uninstalling the app.
  • Data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies (section 3). We cannot access or delete it for you; you can reset your advertising ID and change your ad choices on your device.
  • E-mails you send us: ask us at any time to delete them.

9. Children

CleanSwipe does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob as described in section 3.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at alhadidi.apps@gmail.com to use these rights. We hold no personal data from the app itself; data used for ads is held by Google (section 3). Where an ad-consent choice applies, you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices (ads)".
