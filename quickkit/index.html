QuickKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Publisher: Al-Hadidi Apps · Contact: alhadidi.apps@gmail.com

QuickKit is a set of file tools: reduce photo file size, resize photos, convert to JPG, photos to PDF, PDF to images, merge PDFs and scan a document. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your files are processed on your phone and are never uploaded. The app shows ads from Google, as described below. It has no account, and we, the publisher, receive no data from the app itself.

1. Your files

  • No storage permission. QuickKit asks for no photo, video or storage permission. It reads only the files you choose in Android's photo picker or file picker, or share to it from another app.
  • On-device processing. Every tool runs on your phone. QuickKit never uploads your photos, PDFs, scans, file names or their contents to us or anyone else.
  • Your originals stay unchanged. QuickKit never modifies, moves or deletes the files you pick. New files are saved to Pictures/QuickKit or Download/QuickKit when you tap Save, or handed to the app you choose when you share.
  • Temporary files. While a task runs, working copies are kept in the app's private cache. They are deleted when you cancel or dismiss the result; files you shared are kept up to 24 hours so the receiving app can still read them, then removed.
  • Photo details (metadata). New photos start with no hidden metadata. QuickKit adds back only the camera make and model, the date taken (a setting, on by default) and the location (a setting, off by default). Other hidden data in the original is not carried over. PDFs made from photos contain no photo metadata.

2. Document scanner (Google Play services)

"Scan a document" uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, a component of Google Play services on your phone. It opens the camera, finds the page edges and returns the scan to QuickKit as a PDF. QuickKit itself does not access the camera and uploads nothing. Google Play services may download the scanner's components the first time you use it. Google's handling of data for this component is described in Google's terms for ML Kit: developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

QuickKit is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your choice with Google's consent tool before ads are requested. Where such a choice applies, Settings shows "Privacy choices (ads)" so you can change it at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. On Android versions that support it, Google's ads software may also use Android's own ad services (the Privacy Sandbox, for example ad topics and ad measurement), which you can manage in the ad privacy section of your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. No analytics or crash reporting in this version

This version of QuickKit contains no analytics, crash-reporting or remote-configuration software (such as Google Firebase). The app sends no usage statistics or crash reports to us or to anyone else. If a future version adds such a service, we will update this policy, including what is collected and how long it is kept, before that version is released.

5. Contacting us

If you write to us at alhadidi.apps@gmail.com, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write, and we use them to reply to you. The app pre-fills the subject with its name and version number; nothing else is attached. You can ask us to delete our correspondence with you at any time. The Facebook and Instagram links in the app's "About & contact" section open those services, whose own privacy policies apply.

6. What QuickKit does not do

  • No account or sign-in; we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No access to your location, contacts, microphone or messages; QuickKit has no camera permission (the scanner's camera screen belongs to Google Play services).
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Everything in the app works offline. Without a connection, ads simply do not load.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: temporary files are removed automatically (section 1); app settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. Files you saved are yours and stay until you delete them.
  • Data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies (section 3). We cannot access or delete it for you; you can reset your advertising ID and change your ad choices on your device.
  • E-mails you send us: ask us at any time to delete them.

9. Children

QuickKit does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob as described in section 3.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at alhadidi.apps@gmail.com to use these rights. We hold no personal data from the app itself; data used for ads is held by Google (section 3). Where an ad-consent choice applies, you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices (ads)".
