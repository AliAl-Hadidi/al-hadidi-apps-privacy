Iraqi Translator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Iraqi Translator / مترجم اللهجة العراقية (ali.alhadidi.Iraqi_Translator), version 2.0 · Publisher: Al-Hadidi Apps · Contact: alhadidi.apps@gmail.com

In short: the dictionary works on your device and needs no account. The app shows ads (Google AdMob). If ad consent allows it and the setting is on, it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase). If you choose to send a word suggestion, it is stored with a random anonymous ID so we can review it.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Your favourites, translation history, learning progress and settings.
  • A copy of the suggestions you wrote, so you can see their status.

We cannot access this data. It is deleted when you clear the history in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

2. Text from a photo and voice input

  • "Text from a photo" reads English text from a picture you choose or take, on your device, using Google ML Kit (provided through Google Play services). The app does not upload or keep the picture.
  • Voice input uses your device's speech recognition service. Depending on your device, that service may process audio on Google's servers under Google's terms. The app does not record or keep audio.
  • Listening to a word uses your device's text-to-speech engine.

3. Dictionary updates

About every 15 days, or when you tap "Check for dictionary updates", the app reads the public dictionary from Google Cloud Firestore. No personal data is sent beyond what every network request contains, such as your IP address.

4. Word suggestions ("Translate with us")

Only if you tap Send, the app:

  • signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously. This creates a random user ID; no name, email or phone number is involved.
  • stores your suggestion in Google Cloud Firestore: the words and note you typed, the random ID, the app version and the time.
  • keeps a small record linked to the random ID with the time and number of your recent suggestions. This limits abuse to at most one suggestion a minute and 20 a day.

Only our reviewers can read suggestions. Approved words may be added to a future dictionary version without any ID. We keep suggestions and these records until they are no longer needed for the dictionary, or until you ask us to delete them by emailing alhadidi.apps@gmail.com.

5. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

When ad consent allows it (or no consent is required in your region) and the setting "Share usage and crash data" is on (it is on by default), the app sends:

  • anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, such as screens used, app version, device model and an app-instance identifier;
  • crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics.

We use these only to improve the app. You can turn the setting off at any time in Settings.

6. Advertising: Google AdMob

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information and ad interactions. Ads may be personalised according to your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Security, sharing and children

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the Google services named above, which process it for us or under their own terms.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Questions or deletion requests: alhadidi.apps@gmail.com.
